KEY PRINCIPLE: KEPLER’S THIRD LAW

A farther planet takes longer to orbit

Kepler's third law connects a planet's average distance from the Sun to its orbital period. In solar-system units, the cube of the distance in astronomical units is approximately the square of the period in Earth years: a³ ≈ T².
