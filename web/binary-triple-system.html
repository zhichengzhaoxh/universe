THE KEY IDEA

The barycenter is the system's balance point

Gravity pulls every star toward every other star. Their motions respond to the whole group, so even the more massive star moves around the shared center of mass. With three stars, small differences in starting conditions can lead to very different paths.

About this model

This educational animation integrates Newton's law of gravity for point masses using a fourth-order Runge–Kutta method. Distances, masses, and time are in normalized units; the presets illustrate orbital patterns, not specific observed stars. Real systems can be affected by additional bodies, stellar size, and relativistic effects. The three-body problem has no general closed-form solution.

Educational simulation — not an ephemeris or a long-term stability prediction.
